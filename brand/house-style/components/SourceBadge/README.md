A small pill that says where a number came from.

`Procella.SourceBadge({kind, text})`, with `kind` one of `live`, `cached`, `crosscheck`, `model`.

- `live`: fetched from NASA POWER in this session. `cached`: a committed snapshot; always include its date. `crosscheck`: two independent sources agree on sign and significance (MERRA-2 and IMERG). `model`: reanalysis or model-derived values.
- Every chart plate and dossier carries at least one badge. Never show "Live" when the value came from a snapshot.
- On a plate the badge switches to plate colours automatically.
