A news lead the traveller clipped: the question that starts an investigation.

`Procella.Dispatch({dateline, headline, standfirst, cta, href})`.

- The headline is a question the data can answer, never a claim: "Is the sky over Delhi getting darker?"
- The standfirst says what the record covers (years, cell, source). It never states the result; the dossier does.
- `cta` is the only lantern element on the card. "Open the dossier" is the default wording.
- Use real places from the screening table. Do not quote or paraphrase a real news article.
