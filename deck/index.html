02 · Our challenge

Pick a variable. Pick a place. Prove the trend.

Earth’s environment changes all the time. When a measurement keeps moving one way, rising or falling, that is a trend. One cause can push a variable up in one region and down in another.

Each answer names four things:

WHAT

WHERE

HOW MUCH

SIGNIFICANT?

Significant is the scientist’s word for “unlikely to be random noise”. The audience: high-school and college students, and curious adults.

OBJECTIVE 01

Identify variables worth investigating

Temperature, rainfall, soil wetness, sunlight and greenness, each explained in plain words.

OBJECTIVE 02

Acquire the variable for one region

The user picks a place. Most trends are regional.

OBJECTIVE 03

Build a time series long enough to test

Monthly or daily data, ideally 20 years or more, drawn as a chart.

OBJECTIVE 04

Report a rate and a significance test

Sen’s slope gives the rate per year. Mann-Kendall says if it is significant.

04 · How we plan to solve it

A destination dossier for every trip.

Read a news lead, pick a destination, open its dossier. The evidence comes first, the story second.

Destination dossier

one grid cell · annual means

01 Chart plate

The raw series first, then the Sen’s slope line. Units and source on every plate.

02 Rate

Sen’s slope: how far the value moves per year, in its own units.

03 Verdict

The Mann-Kendall test, read as one of three honest stamps.

Detected

p below 0.05

Not detectable yet

p of 0.05 or more; a longer record may change it

Too short to judge

fewer than 15 years of data

04 Trust check

Re-run from other start years. If the verdict flips, the dossier says so.

Procella · Method: Mann-Kendall, Sen’s slope

04 / 07

Every destination gets a dossier. The data comes first: a chart plate with the raw series, then the rate per year from Sen’s slope, then a Mann-Kendall verdict with three honest states. Detected means p below 0.05. Not detectable yet means no significant trend at this record length. Too short to judge means fewer than 15 years. A trust check re-runs the test from other start years, because some verdicts flip. Only then does a fixed template write the story, so nothing is invented. Each quest maps to one challenge objective, and the finale is the Twin Case: the same variable trending in opposite directions in two places. We claim opposite only when both confidence intervals exclude zero.
